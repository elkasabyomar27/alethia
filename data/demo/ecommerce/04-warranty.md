# Helios Home — Warranty

Fictional demonstration policy. Version: 2026-10-26.

## Eligible product and duration

The Aster Desk Lamp carries a limited 12-month warranty from the purchase date covering manufacturing defects. Keep proof of purchase. The Thalassa Bottle and Olive Notebook do not have an additional commercial warranty under this fictional policy. Damage or defects on arrival should follow the returns policy.

## Exclusions and claims

Accidental damage, liquid damage, misuse, unauthorized repairs, and normal wear are excluded from the lamp warranty. Support must review the receipt and fault description before approving a claim. Do not promise approval, a particular repair time, or a replacement without that review. The policy does not define a fixed repair turnaround or out-of-warranty repair price.
