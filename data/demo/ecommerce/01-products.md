- Aster Desk Lamp (ASTER-L01): EGP 850. LED desk lamp with three brightness levels, USB-C cable, and a 12-month limited warranty. Wall adapter is not included.

This catalog does not provide live stock levels, discounts, or order status. Refer requests for these details to a support agent.
